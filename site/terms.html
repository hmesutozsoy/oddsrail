USING ODDSRAIL

Terms of use

Hosted live trading and prize competitions are not currently available.

What the service provides

OddsRail offers market information and strategy configuration. A draft is not an active trading agent. The website has no paper-trading controls. Self-hosted live execution is a separate installation that the operator configures and controls.

Trading and simulations

Prediction-market trading can lose your entire trading allocation. Prices and market data may be delayed or unavailable. Simulated results omit fees, queue priority and market impact, and do not establish likely live performance. Entry limits and sampled stop rules do not guarantee a maximum loss or exit price.

Permissions and accounts

Connecting a wallet identifies its address. It does not by itself grant trading permission. Do not enter an owner wallet private key or a consumer AI account password into the website. A future hosted trading service will require a separate description of permissions, signing-key handling, revocation and availability before activation.

Responsible use

Follow the applicable venue rules and eligibility restrictions. Being able to view OddsRail does not establish eligibility to trade. Do not use the service for deceptive activity, artificial trading volume, unauthorized account access or attempts to bypass service limits. OddsRail's builder status does not mean that Polymarket guarantees its strategies, security, or results.

AI and competitions

Running the built-in strategy rules does not require ongoing model calls. Hosted AI research is not enabled in this build. Any future included allowance must show when usage becomes unavailable. Competition dates, eligibility, scoring, prizes and sponsors will be published separately before entry opens.

Privacy and support

See the privacy information for the existing hosted service. New strategy drafts are saved in this browser. Use the support tracker for product issues. Do not put credentials or private account details in public issues.

Runs the switches on our server against the live Polymarket book, into a paper ledger that belongs to this browser. No account, nothing installed. Every decision comes back with its check_order verdict.

What one pass is, and is not
  • One tick, now. A pass reads the book at this moment and decides once. It is not a backtest and does not replay past days.
  • The ledger remembers. Positions, resting quotes and fills from earlier passes are loaded, re-marked at the current mid, and the risk rules are applied to them before anything new is placed.
  • Signals look back, orders do not. Fade and momentum read the last hours of prices to judge a move; they only act on what is happening now.
  • Nothing is watched between passes. A resting quote fills and a stop loss triggers only when the next pass looks. Run again to advance the clock; hourly scheduling on our server is the next step.

What the switches cannot do

  
    
    
  • Stop losses are review rules, not orders. Polymarket has no stop orders. A stop here means: at each review, if the mark is below your threshold, sell at the bid. Between reviews nothing protects you.
    • 
    
  • Paper is an upper bound. Fills are simulated from the live book with no queue, no impact and no fees, and paper does not pay out at resolution; it marks at the mid.
    • 
    
  • Two-sided quoting looked fine on paper for us too. The maintainer's own market-making engine lost about 4.5 cents per share on markout live at small size. That fragment ships with the warning inside the prompt.
    • 
    
  • One pass per message. Claude runs the prompt once. For an agent that keeps going, run the same prompt on a schedule with the self-hosted plugin, or ask Claude for another pass. In Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http oddsrail https://mcp.oddsrail.app/mcp.

What the switches cannot do

  • Stop losses are review rules, not orders. Polymarket has no stop orders. A stop here means: at each review, if the mark is below your threshold, sell at the bid. Between reviews nothing protects you.
  • Paper is an upper bound. Fills are simulated from the live book with no queue, no impact and no fees, and paper does not pay out at resolution; it marks at the mid.
  • Two-sided quoting looked fine on paper for us too. The maintainer's own market-making engine lost about 4.5 cents per share on markout live at small size. That fragment ships with the warning inside the prompt.
  • One pass per message. Claude runs the prompt once. For an agent that keeps going, run the same prompt on a schedule with the self-hosted plugin, or ask Claude for another pass. In Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http oddsrail https://mcp.oddsrail.app/mcp.
